OpenSpeedcam community point layer (snapshot 2026-11-12): static enforcement
points of the Kaluga region from the freely editable open database
OpenSpeedcam (https://openspeedcam.net/). The project states that all points
are available for correction, update and download. Points are imported with
attribution and a per-point source link; mobile ambushes, typical police
locations and hazard POIs are excluded at import. Community positions are
never merged into OSM or official records.
